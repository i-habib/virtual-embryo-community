Limits

  • One pseudo pair per side, heart only. The validation step is 0.5 days, with 2 input stages, and the test step is 0.75 days, with 2 input stages. Real steps are 1 day (validation) and 2 days (test).
  • Each stage was subsampled to 4000 cells. Headlines are noisier than on full stages, and 3 seeds give little information about seed noise.
  • Candidate families are simple shifts, resamplings and rescalings. A real submission uses a learned model, so these rankings do not transfer one-for-one to model rankings.
  • Degraded controls shrink each cell toward the per-gene mean of the predicted cells. This is one reading of 'shrunk toward the population mean'.
  • Local anchors come from split-half scores within one pseudo target. They are not the organisers' ceilings.

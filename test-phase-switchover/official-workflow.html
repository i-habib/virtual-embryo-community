Test-phase switchover: official workflow

How a team moves from validation-phase submissions to the two official test submissions per board in the Virtual Embryo Challenge. This is a community guide, not an official document. Facts come from the official pages as read on 14 October 2026 and from the linked tool READMEs. The organisers' pages are authoritative, so re-read the timeline and rules on 20 October.

Items marked not stated do not appear in the official text read for this guide. Ask the organisers before relying on them.

1. Timeline

  1. 14 Oct 2026 Now: validation phase The leaderboard shows one headline number per file. Pin the scorer version and set up the disclosure process before 19 October. Source: test-phase README
  2. 20 Oct 2026 Test phase starts Validation answers are released. Test inputs come out without labels, and the test leaderboard opens. First week to 27 October: check files and choose two per board. Source: tasks page, timeline page
  3. 2 Dec 2026 Final submissions due The last moment a submission can be made or a final entry nominated. Nothing after this counts, in either track. The evidence deadline for Agent Team entries: not stated; ask the organisers. Source: timeline page
  4. 4 Dec 2026 Official evaluation starts Evaluation runs on the hidden test sets. Agent Team entries are checked against the evidence attached to them. Source: timeline page
  5. 11 Dec 2026 Winners announced Announced at NeurIPS. Final rankings come from the hidden test sets, not the development leaderboard. A written method report is due within 14 days of notification. Source: rules sections 13 and 15

4. Rules to keep in view

  • A captain may change track before the final deadline. Doing so retires every score the team has earned (rules section 8).
  • Keep your own copy of everything you submit (rules section 12).
  • Any use of the data in a publication or public presentation needs prior approval from Dr. Neil Chi and the data-generating team (FAQ).
  • Format checks are unlimited in every phase and spend nothing (rules section 11). The checker here does not replace the organisers' upload check or scorer.

5. Open items: ask the organisers
